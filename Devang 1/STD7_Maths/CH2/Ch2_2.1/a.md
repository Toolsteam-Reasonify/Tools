## Learn Mode

### 1. Rakesh's Puzzle: A Number Game

**Challenge 1:**
Find two numbers whose sum is 25 and difference is 11.

**Method:**
Try different pairs and check:
1. Do the two numbers add up to 25?
2. Is the difference between them 11?

**Example guesses:**

| First Number | Second Number | Sum | Difference |
|--------------|---------------|-----|------------|
| 10           | 15            | 25  | -5         |
| 20           | 5             | 25  | 15         |
| 19           | 6             | 25  | 13         |
| 18           | 7             | 25  | **11** ✓   |

**Answer:** 18 and 7

**Challenge 2:**
Find two numbers whose sum is 25 but difference is -11.

**Hint:** If you swap the numbers from Challenge 1, you get 7 and 18!

**Key Insight:**
Swapping numbers changes the sign of the difference but keeps the sum the same.

### 2. Carrom Coin Integers

**Concept:**
Model integer operations using a coin moving on a number line.

**Setup:**
- The coin starts at position 0
- Rightward movement = positive integers
- Leftward movement = negative integers

**Formula:**
```
P = a + b
```
Where:
- P = final position
- a = first movement (positive for right, negative for left)
- b = second movement (positive for right, negative for left)

**Example 1:**
- First strike: 5 units right (a = 5)
- Second strike: 7 units left (b = -7)
- Final position: P = 5 + (-7) = -2

The coin is 2 units to the left of 0.

**Example 2:**
- First movement: -4
- Final position: 5
- Question: What is the second movement?

Solution: -4 + b = 5, so b = 9

**Practice Questions:**
1. If the first movement is -4 and the final position is 5, what is the second movement?
2. If there are multiple strikes: 1, -2, 3, -4, ..., -10, what is the final position?

### 3. Token Model

**Concept:**
- Green token (●) = positive 1
- Red token (●) = negative 1
- One green + one red = zero pair (they cancel out)

**Example: Finding (+7) - (+18)**

**Step 1:** Start with 7 positive (green) tokens
```
● ● ● ● ● ● ●
```

**Step 2:** We need to remove 18 positives, but we only have 7!
- Add 11 zero pairs (11 green + 11 red)
- Now we have: 18 green tokens and 11 red tokens

**Step 3:** Remove 18 green tokens

**Step 4:** What's left? 11 red tokens = -11

**Result:** 7 - 18 = -11

### 4. Key Properties

**Additive Inverse:**
- The additive inverse of an integer a is -a
- Additive inverse of 18: -(18) = -18
- Additive inverse of -18: -(-18) = 18

**Important Identity:**
Subtracting a number = Adding its additive inverse
- 7 - 18 = 7 + (-18)
- 4 - (-12) = 4 + 12

## Real World Mode

### 1. Temperature Changes 🌡️

**Scenario:**
A freezing process requires that the room temperature be lowered at the rate of 5°C every hour.

**How Integers Help:**
- Starting temperature: positive integer (e.g., 8°C)
- Temperature decrease: negative integer (-5°C per hour)
- Formula: Final Temperature = Starting Temperature + (Hours × -5)

**Example:**
- Starting: 8°C
- After 4 hours: 8 + (4 × -5) = 8 + (-20) = -12°C

**Real Applications:**
- Industrial freezing processes
- Weather forecasting
- Climate control systems
- Food preservation

---

### 2. Bank Transactions 💰

**Scenario:**
Track your bank account balance using integers.

**How Integers Help:**
- Deposits: positive integers (+)
- Withdrawals: negative integers (-)
- Formula: Final Balance = Starting Balance + Deposits - Withdrawals

**Example:**
- Starting balance: ₹1000
- Deposit: ₹500
- Withdrawal: ₹300
- Final: 1000 + 500 - 300 = ₹1200

**Real Applications:**
- Personal finance management
- Business accounting
- Credit card statements
- Investment tracking

---

### 3. Elevation and Sea Level 🏔️

**Scenario:**
Sea level is considered as 0. Heights above are positive, depths below are negative.

**How Integers Help:**
- Above sea level: positive integers (e.g., +8849m for Mt. Everest)
- Below sea level: negative integers (e.g., -430m for Dead Sea)
- Formula: Final Position = Starting Position + Change

**Real Examples:**
- Mount Everest: +8,849 meters (highest point on Earth)
- Dead Sea shore: -430 meters (lowest land elevation)
- Mariana Trench: -11,034 meters (deepest ocean point)
- Submarine at 500m depth: -500 meters

**Real Applications:**
- Aviation and flight planning
- Submarine navigation
- Geological surveys
- Topographic mapping

### 4. More Real-World Applications

#### Sports Scoring 🏈
In golf, scores are relative to par (0):
- Under par: negative (good) → -3 means 3 under par
- Over par: positive → +2 means 2 over par

#### Time Zones 🕐
Time zones relative to UTC (0):
- East of UTC: positive → India UTC+5:30
- West of UTC: negative → New York UTC-5

#### Stock Market 📊
Price changes:
- Gains: positive → +15 (gained ₹15)
- Losses: negative → -8 (lost ₹8)

#### Building Floors 🏢
Floor numbering:
- Ground floor: 0
- Above ground: positive → 5th floor (+5)
- Basement: negative → Basement 2 (-2)

#### Video Games 🎮
Game mechanics:
- Health gained: positive → +50 health
- Damage taken: negative → -20 health
- Score bonuses: positive → +100 points
- Penalties: negative → -25 points

#### Historical Dates 📅
Years in history:
- Before Christ (BC): negative → 500 BC = -500
- After Christ (AD): positive → 2025 AD = +2025

## Key Terms

- **Integer**: A whole number that can be positive, negative, or zero
- **Sum**: The result of adding two or more numbers
- **Difference**: The result of subtracting one number from another (first - second)
- **Number Line**: A visual representation of numbers on a straight line
- **Additive Inverse**: The number that, when added to a given number, results in zero
- **Zero Pair**: A pair of one positive and one negative token that equal zero
- **Token Model**: A visual model using colored tokens to represent integers
- **Magnitude**: The absolute value or size of a number, without regard to its sign
- **Direction**: Whether a number is positive (right/up) or negative (left/down)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ Forgetting that difference means first - second (order matters)
2. ❌ Not recognizing that subtracting is like adding the inverse
3. ❌ Missing negative signs in calculations
4. ❌ Confusing magnitude with direction on the number line
5. ❌ Not checking both sum and difference in puzzles
6. ❌ Forgetting to change signs when finding additive inverse
7. ❌ Mixing up deposits and withdrawals in banking problems
8. ❌ Confusing above/below in elevation problems
